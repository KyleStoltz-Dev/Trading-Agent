# Playbook v0 — hypotheses to measure

This is a starting taxonomy extracted from prior charts and narrative. It is not yet a
validated edge. Definitions must become observable enough that two reviews can reach
substantially the same classification.

## Mindset contract

Inspired by the Mark Douglas principles shown in the source material:

- Anything can happen.
- A trader does not need certainty about the next outcome to execute an edge.
- Wins and losses can be randomly distributed within an edge.
- An edge is a probabilistic indication, not a prediction.
- Each market moment is unique.
- Risk is predefined and accepted before entry.
- Process quality and outcome quality are scored separately.

## Three-layer decision model

### 1. Context

- Higher-timeframe structure: last protected higher low or lower high.
- Candidate accumulation, distribution, reaccumulation, or redistribution.
- Location within a defined dealing range or point of interest.
- Market regime: trend, range, expansion, contraction, or event-driven.
- External liquidity candidates such as equal highs/lows.
- Scheduled news and session context.

### 2. Trigger

- Liquidity sweep with a precisely defined reference level.
- Displacement or imbalance with minimum measurable magnitude.
- Break of structure with an explicit swing-definition rule.
- Mitigation/retest of the initiating area.
- Lower-timeframe confirmation (initial candidates: 15m, 5m, 1m).

Dropping timeframe is for refining a pre-existing higher-timeframe thesis. It must not be
used to invent a new thesis after the original invalidates.

### 3. Management

- Predefined initial risk, including spread and fees.
- Initial minimum target candidate: 3R; this remains a hypothesis to test.
- Partial taken, quantity, price, and reason.
- Runner rules and trailing/invalidation method.
- Hedge considered, hedge taken, and whether it changed net directional exposure.
- Maximum favorable/adverse excursion after each management decision.

## Definitions that still need operationalization

- Accumulation/distribution and nested reaccumulation.
- “80% of accumulation”: exact range anchors and calculation.
- Institutional candle and mitigation.
- Fair-value gap/imbalance and minimum size.
- Manipulation versus ordinary liquidity sweep.
- Equal highs/lows tolerance.
- Break of structure swing sensitivity.
- POI freshness and expiration.

## Regime adaptation

Do not rewrite rules based on the latest few trades. Segment results by:

- instrument and venue;
- volatility percentile or ATR regime;
- directional versus rotational session;
- session and minutes from major news;
- context and trigger timeframe pair;
- setup version.

Promote a rule change only after recording the previous version, sample size, hypothesis,
and expected improvement.
