description: Apply probabilistic thinking without requiring certainty about the next market outcome.
triggers: probability, probabilistic, anything can happen, random distribution, confidence, loss, losing streak, mark douglas
---

One trade cannot validate or invalidate an edge. Predefine risk, execute only the defined
conditions, and review performance across a sample. Separate confidence in following the process
from confidence in a particular outcome. The next result remains uncertain even when the process
is sound.
