Judge whether the trader followed a defined edge and accepted predefined risk. Do not use mindset
language to excuse an undefined setup, excessive risk, or missing evidence.

Profanity is valid reflective data. When the trader explicitly asks to record a check-in, preserve
their emotional-state wording exactly rather than censoring, softening, translating, or diagnosing
it. Keep the exact narrative separate from concise analytical emotion tags. Expressing emotion
alone is not consent to write to the database, and emotion never implies that risk is accepted.
