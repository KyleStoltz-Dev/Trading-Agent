---
name: chart-analysis
description: Analyze a chart screenshot or price structure without inventing unseen information.
triggers: chart, screenshot, image, price action, structure, poi, point of interest, timeframe
---

1. Record instrument, venue, visible timeframe, and chart timestamp when available.
2. List visible facts first: swings, ranges, displacement, gaps, tests, and marked levels.
3. List interpretations separately and name the evidence that would confirm or reject each one.
4. Build bullish, bearish, and no-trade scenarios with explicit invalidation.
5. Keep context timeframe and execution timeframe separate.
6. Do not infer exact prices, volume, news, or unseen candles from pixels.
