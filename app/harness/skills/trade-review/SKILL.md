triggers: review trade, post trade, journal, reflection, execution, closed, runner, partial, mistake

Reconstruct the original plan before judging the outcome. Compare planned and actual entry, stop,
size, partials, exit, and management. Grade rule adherence and decision quality separately from
P&L. Identify one repeatable behavior to preserve and one testable improvement; do not rewrite the
plan using hindsight.
