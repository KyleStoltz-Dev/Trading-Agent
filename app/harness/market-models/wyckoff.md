description: Observable tests for accumulation, distribution, springs, upthrusts, and range behavior.
triggers: wyckoff, accumulation, distribution, spring, upthrust, utad, reaccumulation, redistribution
---

Start with the trading range boundaries, prior trend, attempts to leave the range, and response
after each attempt. A spring or upthrust is provisional until price demonstrates rejection and
follow-through. Nested ranges are possible, but each claimed range needs its own boundaries,
timeframe, invalidation, and evidence.
